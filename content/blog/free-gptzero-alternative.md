GPTZero is the AI detector most students try first, and the free tier runs out. After a few checks you are asked to pay, or the word limit cuts the essay in half. You do not need a subscription to see which sentences look AI-written before you submit.

The [DocMaxxing AI detector](/ai-detector) is free for that preview: paste the draft, get a score, and see the lines that raised it. No account for a short text. It is our own model, trained against real Turnitin reports, so the highlights are there to be rewritten — not to stand in for the official Turnitin number.

## What you are replacing

- **Price.** GPTZero's free tier is a few checks, then a paywall. The DocMaxxing detector checks a short text for free, with no account.
- **What you see.** Both show a score and the sentences behind it. Ours marks the lines that look AI-like so you know what to rewrite.
- **Turnitin.** Neither one is Turnitin. Ours is tuned on real Turnitin reports and is still a preview: the two numbers will not match one-for-one.
- **The next step.** GPTZero leaves you to copy the text out and edit it somewhere else. Here you rewrite the highlights, or send a long passage to the [humanizer](/humanizer), then check again.

ZeroGPT, Winston, Copyleaks and the rest of the free checkers disagree with each other and with Turnitin. Picking the one that gives you the lowest number is not a plan. Pick the one that shows the sentences, fix those, and check again.

## How to check an essay before you submit

1. Paste the whole draft, at least a few hundred words. A single paragraph is too little for a stable score.
2. Read the highlights, not just the percentage. The percentage is the share of prose that looks machine-written. The highlights are the lines you can actually change.
3. Rewrite them in your own rhythm, or run a long flagged stretch through the [AI humanizer](/humanizer). Keep citations, numbers and headings as they are, and read every rewritten sentence.
4. Check once more. If the same paragraph lights up, that paragraph still has the template shape — a synonym swap will not move it. [How to bring a Turnitin AI score down](/blog/how-to-bypass-turnitin-ai-detection) walks through why.

Turnitin itself remains the official report at schools that use it. Students often cannot open the AI view, which is the reason for a preview at all. What the official number means in a teacher's hands is covered in [what is a good Turnitin AI score](/blog/what-is-a-good-turnitin-ai-score).

## What a free checker will not do

It will not match Turnitin's percentage exactly. The two models see the same kind of pattern and still land on different figures. It will not clear a draft that is an untouched chatbot answer: the highlights will cover almost every sentence, and the fix is rewriting, not a second checker. And it will not make a course allow AI. If the assignment forbids it, a low score is not permission.

## Key takeaways

- GPTZero's free tier is a short trial. The check you need before a deadline has to be repeatable.
- Use the [free AI detector](/ai-detector) to see the sentences, rewrite those, and run it again.
- Treat every free checker, including ours, as a preview of Turnitin — not as Turnitin.
