1. Digital goods

Credits and Assignment payments are digital goods. Once credits are consumed by successful paid actions, those spent credits are generally non-refundable as cash.

2. Automatic credit refunds

If a paid action fails on our side (provider error, crash after debit, failed packaging with no usable output), we refund the charged credits to your DocMaxxing balance whenever automatic recovery runs.

3. Unused credit purchases

Unused credit package purchases may be eligible for a refund within 14 days of purchase if credits have not been substantially consumed. Contact support with:

  • Account email
  • Approximate purchase time
  • Paddle transaction id

4. Assignment project payments

Assignment pricing is shown before you confirm. If the pipeline never starts because of a billing failure, you should not be charged. If you were charged and received no usable delivery after reasonable retries, contact support for review.

5. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback. Unjustified chargebacks may result in account suspension and loss of remaining credits.

6. How to request a refund

Email support@docmaxxing.com or WhatsApp +998 99 077 99 09 (Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM GMT+5).
