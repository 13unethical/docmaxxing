Getting started

Do I need an account? You can try Format as a guest for many flows, but buying credits, saving Assignment progress, and paid Workspace actions require Sign up.

Where do I buy credits? Read the Credits guide, then open Buy credits. Guests are asked to register before checkout.

What are welcome credits? New accounts receive a welcome bonus so you can try paid Workspace actions before purchasing.

Format

What files can I upload? Briefs and text as paste or DOC/PDF/TXT/images (as shown on the Format page).

Which styles are supported? Harvard, APA, MLA presets plus custom font, size, spacing, margins, cover page, references, and citations.

Humanizer

How does humanizing run? Assignment humanizing uses StealthWriter Legacy 5.1 in large batches (up to ~5,000 words), not tiny paragraph-by-paragraph API spam.

Why is AI score still high? Later revision / repair steps can rewrite prose. The pipeline re-humanizes after those steps; always re-check the final DOCX on your detector of choice.

Assignment

What is the pipeline? Upload brief → price → pay → research → blueprint → write sections → citations → humanize → format → review/revision → validation → AI detection → delivery ZIP.

Word count rules? Targets use a ±10% band (example: 1200 → 1080–1320). Overages are trimmed (including Gemini smart-trim when needed).

Download says ready but nothing downloads? Use Download again — packaging rebuilds the ZIP. Hard-refresh if the UI was stale.

Turnitin & detection

Is this official Turnitin? We provide Turnitin-style report workflows through configured providers. Institutional Turnitin portals can still differ.

Cost? Similarity check is priced in credits (see Credits page; typically 300 credits per check).

Billing

Who charges my card? Paddle is the payment processor / merchant of record.

Refunds? See Refund Policy.
