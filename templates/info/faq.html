Getting started

Do I need an account? You can try Format as a guest for many flows. Buying credits, saving Assignment progress, running paid Humanizer / Turnitin actions, and Earn & Share require sign-up.

Where do I buy credits? Read the Credits guide, then open Buy credits. Checkout uses Cryptomus (crypto) and/or Paddle (cards). Guests are asked to register before checkout.

What are welcome credits? New accounts receive 50 welcome credits. Signing up with a referral link also grants +100 referral bonus credits.

Format

What files can I upload? Briefs and text as paste or DOC/PDF/TXT/images (as shown on the Format page).

Which styles are supported? Harvard, APA, MLA presets plus custom font, size, spacing, margins, cover page, references, and citations.

Does Format cost credits? No — Format is free.

Humanizer

How does humanizing run? The standalone Humanizer and Assignment humanizing use StealthWriter Legacy 5.1. Assignment batches can run up to ~5,000 words.

Why is AI score still high? Later revision / repair steps can rewrite prose. The pipeline re-humanizes after those steps; always re-check the final DOCX on your detector of choice.

Cost? Typically 10 credits per humanize action (Pro users get 10% off paid charges).

Assignment

What is the pipeline? Upload brief → price → pay → research → blueprint → write sections → citations → humanize → format → review/revision → validation → AI detection → delivery ZIP.

Word count rules? Targets use a ±10% band (example: 1200 → 1080–1320). Overages are trimmed (including Gemini smart-trim when needed).

Download says ready but nothing downloads? Use Download again — packaging rebuilds the ZIP. Hard-refresh if the UI was stale.

Turnitin & detection

Is this official Turnitin? We provide Turnitin-style report workflows through configured providers. Institutional Turnitin portals can still differ.

Cost? Typically 300 credits per check. Earn & Share milestones can award free detailed Turnitin reports.

Earn & Share

How does cashback work? When someone you referred deposits, you earn 40% of that USD amount into your referral balance.

What counts as a qualifying friend? Their first deposit of $10+. Milestones unlock free Turnitin reports, bonus credits, and Pro (10% off) at 10 qualifying friends.

Convert or withdraw? Convert referral USD to credits at 1.2×, or request a withdrawal from $50 minimum (manual review).

Coming soon

What about Check, Workspace, and Presentation? They are listed in the menu but currently show a Soon page. They are not live yet.

Billing

Who processes payment? Cryptomus for crypto checkout (when configured) and Paddle for card / wallet payments (merchant of record).

Refunds? See Refund Policy.
