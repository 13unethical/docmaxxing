DocMaxxing helps students and researchers ship clean academic documents without fighting Word styles for hours. The product combines free formatting, an AI humanizer, Turnitin-style reports, a document editor, a presentation maker, citations, a full Assignment pipeline, and a referral cashback program in one place.

What you can do today

  • Humanizer — rewrite stiff, AI-sounding prose into natural writing with our own fine-tuned model and a partner rewriting engine. Short texts are free every day; see the AI Humanizer page.
  • Format — paste or upload text, apply Harvard / APA / MLA / Chicago / IEEE presets, fonts, margins, cover page, references, and citations, then download a DOCX. Free.
  • Workspace — a document editor: write or paste your paper, humanize a selection, review suggested changes, and export a DOCX.
  • Slides — turn a topic or your paper into a presentation, pick a template, and download a PowerPoint file.
  • AI check — run Turnitin-style similarity and AI report workflows billed in credits.
  • Assignment — upload a brief, review the price, pay once, then auto-run research → blueprint → writing → citations → formatting → review → delivery ZIP.
  • Work Review (Academic Check) — compare a draft to your assignment brief and rubric (free).
  • Veil — a Chrome extension that explains and humanizes text on the page you are reading.
  • Earn & Share — invite friends, earn cashback on their deposits, unlock milestone rewards, convert to credits, or withdraw.

How Assignment works

The Assignment pipeline treats your brief as the source of truth. We extract required sections, word budgets (±10%), formatting rules, rubric / learning outcomes, and mandatory evidence (for example lecture/seminar references in Learning Journals). Writing happens section by section, and a grade gate repairs word count and rubric gaps before export.

Credits economy

Format stays free, and short humanizer runs are free every day. Paid actions (longer humanizer runs, AI checks, Assignment, Slides and related metered tools) spend credits. Who we are for

Undergraduates, postgraduates, and anyone who needs consistent academic formatting and drafting help — especially when deadlines are tight and briefs are strict.
