DocMaxxing helps students and researchers ship clean academic documents without fighting Word styles for hours. The product combines formatting, AI humanization, integrity checks, citations, and a full assignment pipeline in one place.

What you can do

  • Format — paste or upload a brief + text, apply Harvard / APA / MLA presets, fonts, margins, cover page, references, and citations, then download a DOCX.
  • Humanizer — rewrite AI-sounding prose through StealthWriter (Legacy 5.1) in large batches up to ~5,000 words.
  • Turnitin — run similarity / AI-oriented report workflows with credit-based checks.
  • Assignment — upload a brief, pay once, then auto-run research → blueprint → writing → citations → humanize → format → review → AI detection → delivery ZIP.
  • Check — academic requirement checking against your brief.
  • Workspace — in-editor tools (humanize selections, AI detect highlights, citations) paid with credits.

How Assignment works

The Assignment pipeline is built around your brief as the source of truth. We extract required sections, word budgets (±10%), formatting rules, rubric / learning outcomes, and mandatory evidence (for example lecture/seminar references in Learning Journals). Writing can happen section-by-section; humanizing runs on large batches; a grade gate repairs word count and rubric gaps before export.

Credits economy

Core Format stays free. Academic Check and other paid actions (humanize, AI detect, citation search, Turnitin) spend credits. New accounts receive welcome credits. Packages are sold on the buy credits screen (see also the Credits guide).

Who we are for

Undergraduates, postgraduates, and anyone who needs consistent academic formatting and drafting help — especially when deadlines are tight and briefs are strict.
