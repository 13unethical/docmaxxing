1. Who can pay

You must create a DocMaxxing account before buying credits. Guests can view the Credits page, but checkout requires registration / sign-in.

2. What you pay for

  • Credit packages — prepaid balance for Humanize, AI detect, citations, Turnitin, and similar paid actions.
  • Assignment projects — priced per project after brief analysis (shown before you confirm payment).
  • Free tools — Home Format and Academic Check do not require credits.

3. Processor

Card and wallet payments are processed by Paddle. DocMaxxing does not store full payment card numbers. Paddle may appear on your bank statement as the merchant of record.

4. Prices & currency

Package prices are shown on the Credits page (for example Starter and Pro). Custom / volume deals are arranged via sales on WhatsApp. Taxes or local fees may apply depending on your region and Paddle rules.

5. When credits appear

After a successful Paddle checkout confirmation, credits are added to your account balance. If payment succeeds but balance does not update within a short time, contact support with your account email and Paddle transaction id.

6. Failed paid actions

If a paid feature fails on our side after credits were charged, we refund those credits to your balance whenever automatic recovery is possible.

7. Chargebacks

Unjustified chargebacks may lead to account suspension and forfeiture of remaining credits. Contact us first so we can resolve billing issues quickly.
