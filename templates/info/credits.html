How credits work

Your balance sits on your account. Format does not spend credits. Academic Check (20), Humanize, AI detect, citations, Turnitin, and Assignment projects do. Failed jobs that never run are refunded where the economy ledger supports it.

New accounts receive a welcome bonus (default 50 credits) so you can try Workspace actions before purchasing.

What each action costs

  • Academic Check — 20 credits
  • Humanize — 10 credits
  • AI detect — 10 credits
  • Citation search — 2 credits
  • Turnitin check — 300 credits
  • Assignment projects — priced per brief (word count, priority, extras) before you pay

Packages

  • Starter — 1,000 credits for $9
  • Pro — 2,500 credits for $20 (most popular)
  • Custom / teams — contact sales via WhatsApp or support email

Checkout is handled by Paddle (merchant of record). You need a DocMaxxing account before buying; guests who click Buy are asked to register first.
