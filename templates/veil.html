Core Formula Breakdown: Projectile Trajectories

  • Time of flight: Independent of horizontal velocity; determined entirely by vertical component: T = 2v₀ sinθ / g.
  • Apex height: Kinetic energy from vertical speed converts fully to potential energy: H = v₀² sin²θ / 2g.
  • Symmetry rule: Launch speed equals landing speed in vacuum conditions; flight time up equals flight time down.
  • Veil recommendation: Check whether problem specifies air drag before omitting drag equations.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Veil for Chrome.

How does the panel stay on the page?
Veil draws its panel in its own layer so the page's styles do not restyle it. You drag it where you want, ask a question, and hide it again. It does not open a new tab.
How do the keyboard shortcuts work?
Alt + Q: Toggles the assistant panel open or closed. If you have text selected on the page, it automatically pastes it into the input.
Alt + S: Activates the crosshair crop tool. Click and drag to crop any math formula, chart, or diagram for instant analysis.
Esc: Closes the crop tool. Hide the panel itself with Alt+Q (Option+Q on a Mac).
What does the «Развернутый» toggle do?
When «Развернутый» (Detailed Mode) is checked, Veil generates full step-by-step derivations, theoretical principles, and intermediate math steps. When unchecked, it delivers quick, concise bullet points and direct answers.
Does Veil work on PDF files and slides?
Yes. Veil functions seamlessly over any online PDF, local file opened in Google Chrome, lecture slides on Google Drive, or web-based reading material.
How does Veil connect with my DocMaxxing account?
Sign in from the Veil icon with the same DocMaxxing account you paid on. The {{ "{:,}".format(veil_pass_cost) }} credits buy {{ veil_pass_days }} days of answers. There is no extra charge per question. When the date passes, pay again on this page and Veil starts working again.
