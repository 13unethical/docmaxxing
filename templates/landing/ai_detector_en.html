What an AI score actually means

AI detectors estimate how much of a passage matches patterns common in machine-generated writing. They are statistical tools: they can disagree with each other, change over time, and sometimes flag text a person wrote. Treat the DocMaxxing score as a revision hint, not a classroom verdict. For the official Turnitin AI and similarity report, use the Turnitin check inside the app.
