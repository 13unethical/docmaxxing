What “natural writing” looks like

Made for students, honest about limits

DocMaxxing is built for undergraduates, postgraduates and anyone writing in English for a course: people who draft with AI help where it’s allowed, non-native speakers whose writing comes out stiff, and anyone who would rather spend the evening on the argument than on margins and reference lists.

We don’t promise detector scores. AI detectors are statistical, they disagree with each other, and they sometimes flag work written entirely by people. What we can do is help your text read naturally, keep your formatting right, and show you where a draft falls short of the brief. Always review the result, cite your sources, and follow your institution’s AI policy.
