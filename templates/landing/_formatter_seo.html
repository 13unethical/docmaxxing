What the essay formatter applies

Upload a DOCX or paste your draft, pick a style profile, and generate a submission-ready layout. The formatter does not rewrite your sentences — it sets the document rules your brief asks for.

Page & type

Margins, body font and size, line spacing, and first-line or hanging indents that match the style.

Headings & page numbers

Heading levels and running page numbers (or headers) placed where APA, MLA, Harvard or Chicago expect them.

Cover / title block

Optional title page or title block fields when the style uses them — student name, course, instructor, date.

Citations & references

In-text citation shape and a reference list / Works Cited / bibliography layout for the chosen style.

APA, MLA, Harvard, Chicago — short guides

APA 7

Common for psychology and social sciences. Expects student title-page fields, double spacing, page numbers, Level 1–3 headings, author–date citations, and a References list with hanging indents. The APA preset wires those defaults so you are not hunting through Word styles. How-to cite pilots: website, YouTube, book, journal article.

MLA 9

Standard for many literature and humanities courses. First-page title block instead of a separate cover, double spacing, surname page numbers, and a Works Cited list. Parenthetical citations use author–page where the source has pages. How-to cite pilots: website, YouTube, book.

Harvard

Author–date referencing used widely in UK and Commonwealth universities. Our Harvard preset follows a Cite Them Right-style layout for body text, headings and the reference list — still confirm any local faculty variant your module handbook names. How-to cite pilots: website, book.

Chicago 17

Used across history and some social sciences. Notes-bibliography and author–date variants both appear in briefs; the Chicago preset sets typography, spacing and bibliography layout as a solid starting point you can adjust if your instructor requires notes. How-to cite pilot: website.

IEEE is also available for engineering reports (numbered citations). After formatting, pair the file with the free AI detector or AI humanizer only if your course allows those tools — layout and wording are separate jobs.
